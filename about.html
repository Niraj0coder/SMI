SINCE 1996

Sri Maruthi Industries (SMI)

Sri Maruthi Industries commenced operations in 1996 as a Die/Tool design company, founded by Mr. M.M. Chelladurai, an engineering graduate from Thiagarajar College of Engineering. Over the years, SMI has grown into one of the leading metal stamping companies, specialising in punching, bending, forming and deep drawn stampings.

In 2011, SMI expanded into machining components, and in 2024 the company diversified into fastener products in response to market demand.

SMI is committed to delivering high-quality products, cost-effective solutions and on-time delivery. The company is ISO 9001:2015 and IATF 16949:2016 certified and has been recognised with the 100 PPM and SQ certification by HMIL.

  • 1996 Die / tool design and metal stamping begins
  • 2011 Expansion into machined components
  • 2024 Diversification into fasteners

Certifications & Recognition

Quality systems and approvals that back every component we ship.

100 PPM Certification

Awarded by Hyundai Motor India Ltd. (HMIL)
  • Recognises suppliers with a defect rate of fewer than 100 parts per million (PPM).
  • Demonstrates exceptional product quality and consistent manufacturing excellence.
  • Reflects SMI's commitment to a zero-defect culture, customer satisfaction and continuous improvement.

IATF 16949:2016

International Automotive Quality Management Standard
  • Developed by the International Automotive Task Force (IATF).
  • Integrates with ISO 9001 and focuses on automotive industry requirements.
  • Ensures high product quality, process efficiency and customer satisfaction.
  • Demonstrates SMI's commitment to global quality standards, risk management and continuous improvement.
