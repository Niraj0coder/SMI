OUR STORY

Precision
sheet metal pressed components

Brackets, battery brackets and other pressed parts

SMI began in 1996 as a die and tool design company and has grown into one of the leading metal stamping companies, specialising in punching, bending, forming and deep drawn stampings for OEMs.
